After a long run of particularly awful circumstances and some rather low points I'd rather not go into here, I've made the decision to quit my day job and spend a few months rebooting my life by doing my own thing for a while, working on my own projects and learning some new skills and brushing up on some old ones.

I'm having a workshop built that I intend to use as a studio space, and that'll free up the little self contained building on my property as a room to let, which will keep the wolf from the door during this time.

It's nice feeling better again and having some motivation again. My job over the last few years has kept me near a computer in case anyone has questions or little tasks they need doing, without the job I can drift away from the computer and do messy outdoor things that are completely incompatible with computer use. Or just properly focus on build projects without interruptions. 

So we'll see how this goes. This is a huge turning point and took quite a bit of deliberation to get here, and hopefully it's all positive.
